# Seeing is Believing: the comparison playbook

Saved 3 October 2026 at the owner's request; updated the same day for the scroll-told format. This is the approach that works for the project. Use it for every new page, post, video or update.

## The core idea

Put a familiar yardstick from the reader's own world beside Gaza, in **the same unit**. But first, let the Gaza number hit alone.

- **Your world** is drawn in muted blue. **Gaza** is drawn in red. **What remains** is drawn in grey.
- The meaning is framed as the reader's own family: *your daughter, your son, your home, your family, your mother, your morning.*
- Labels are plain words ("Kindergartens", "Homes"), never riddles.

## Format: scroll-told, one full screen per subject (current)

- **Dark ground throughout.** Each subject is pinned for the whole screen and told in **three steps that replace one another**:
  1. **The number, alone.** It counts up as you scroll, with one plain sentence and the source.
  2. **The comparison.** Inanimate forms only: bars, block grids, week squares.
  3. **What it means.** One line, two or three supporting figures, "What Israel says" where relevant. This step holds the longest, so readers have time to absorb it.
- **Order:**
  1. Intro.
  2. **"Gaza – Rafah from space"**: a full-screen satellite before/after wipe ending on "We let this happen."
  3. The subjects: Children → Kindergartens → School → Homes → Lives → Hospitals → Medical evacuation → Water → Injuries → Bombardment.
  4. Then the companies, "Your choice" and the record.
- **Fixed button:** "Who supplied it ↓" stays on screen until the companies section, for readers who need a way out.
- **Background photos:** Children, Homes and Hospitals carry a full-bleed photo in greyscale at 30% opacity, with a small credit.
- **No pictorial icons.** Testing showed they made the subject feel playful and diluted the number.
- **Type:**
  - TRT Burn for titles: uppercase, line-height 1.1.
  - Anton for numbers.
  - Archivo heavy for statements.
  - No serif and no italic.

## Motion

- **Children:** the counter and the 21,283-square wall climb with the scroll. The climb never eases out early, so readers expect it to stop and watch it keep climbing.
- **Other subjects:** the number counts up, then the bars and blocks draw in step with the scroll, then the meaning holds.
- **Reduced motion:** no pinning, and every state is shown filled.

## Rules that keep it unanswerable

- Never invent or round a figure beyond what the source says. If a source for a desired comparison can't be found, use the closest sourced figure and label it honestly (e.g. kindergartens usable vs children enrolled).
- Each row names its own unit when two rows measure different things.
- Attribute legal findings to the body that made them (ICJ, UN Commission of Inquiry); the page does not declare guilt in its own voice.
- Companies are named with their home-country flag and evidence; logos are not reproduced.
- Background photos carry a credit; a photo used as atmosphere is never captioned as something it doesn't show.
- Images only with a licence on file and verified date, place and publisher. No graphic injuries, and no identifiable children without consent.
- **No pre-written letters or emails.** Acting is the visitor's own moral choice. The site shows how (review investments, move them if they choose, contact companies and brokers in their own words, tell friends and family) and states it is not financial or legal advice.
