# Seeing is Believing: CRO and data-visualisation review

Prepared 3 October 2026. Applied to the live site in the same session.

## The brief (stored as given)

> You are an elite Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) expert, Data Visualisation Designer, and master copywriter. Your goal is to review the landing page metrics provided below and completely re-engineer how they are visually and textually presented on the page.
>
> CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: You must remain 100% faithful to the exact metrics, data points, and sources provided. You must NOT alter the core values, invent new statistics, exaggerate the data, or change the factual basis of the sources. Your job is to improve the PRESENTATION and COMPREHENSION of this existing data for a non-technical audience without losing an ounce of factual accuracy.
>
> The target audience suffers from cognitive overload when looking at raw data. You must make these exact numbers easy to understand, visually compelling, and deeply emotive by explaining their implications clearly.
>
> 1. THE "SO WHAT?" PRESENTATION HIERARCHY — Exact Stat & Source / The Context / The Implication-Emotion.
> 2. IDENTIFY THE "HERO METRIC" LAYOUT — one metric, massive type (72pt+), high contrast, negative space.
> 3. COGNITIVE ANCHORING & COMPARISONS — relatable real-world equivalents, mathematically accurate.
> 4. VISUAL WIREFRAME & UI RECOMMENDATIONS — minimalist component per metric; editorial, not admin dashboard.
>
> Format: "Current Presentation" vs "Optimized Presentation" for each metric, then structural UI/UX recommendations.

Owner's direction added in the same request: remove the "your country" calculator; frame every metric as *your daughter, your home, your family, your future*; let readers write to the companies; the comparison must be seen before it is read.

## 1. The "So what?" hierarchy, metric by metric

### Children killed (HERO)
**Current presentation:** "21,283 of the identified dead were children", one tile among three in the hero.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 21,283 children killed and identified by name. Gaza Ministry of Health, published by UN OCHA, 23 Sep 2026.
- **Context:** Children the Ministry had fully identified to 31 Dec 2025. In the first four months alone, the UN reported more children killed in Gaza (12,300) than in four years of every conflict worldwide (12,193, 2019–2022).
- **Implication:** Enough to fill 851 classrooms of 25. Every dot on the screen is one child.

### Preschool
**Current presentation:** not shown.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 86% of Gaza's kindergartens (563) destroyed, damaged or unreachable (Save the Children, Aug 2026), against 89% of 3–5-year-olds in preschool across rich OECD countries (OECD Family Database, 2023).
- **Context:** Buildings where 3–5-year-olds learned to read and play; about 70,000 young children lost their kindergarten (Theirworld).
- **Implication:** "If your daughter were four, the room where she would learn her letters and make her first friend is gone six times out of seven."
- **Accuracy note:** the two rows measure different things (children enrolled vs buildings usable). Each row is labelled with its own unit on the page.

### School
**Current presentation:** "526 of 564 school buildings need rebuilding" (main page) and a 19-vs-154-weeks dot panel (data page).
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 154 weeks without regular in-person school, from 7 Oct 2023 to the Sep 2026 restart (Reuters), against 19 weeks of COVID closures in England (Institute for Fiscal Studies: 10 + 9 weeks).
- **Context:** 657,200 school-age children need education support (Education Cluster via OCHA), close to every pupil in Scotland (695,923, Pupil Census 2025). 526 of 564 school buildings need rebuilding.
- **Implication:** "A child who was six when the war began is nine now, and has not had one normal school year since."

### Home
**Current presentation:** "Of every 10 homes, fewer than 3 are undamaged."
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 76.6% of homes destroyed or damaged (371,888 of 485,361). UN / EU / World Bank RDNA, Apr 2026; UNOSAT 82% of all structures, 16 Jun 2026.
- **Context:** 65% of households now live in tents or makeshift shelters (Shelter Cluster, Aug 2026).
- **Implication:** "Look down your street." Ten intact houses (your street) above 2.3 (a street in Gaza).

### Family
**Current presentation:** "About 1 in 30 people in Gaza has been killed", 30 hearts.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 73,922 killed, 174,995 injured. Gaza MoH via OCHA, 23 Sep 2026.
- **Context:** 73,922 out of a pre-war population of 2.2 million is 1 in 29.8. 58,000+ children lost one or both parents (UNICEF).
- **Implication:** "Think of 30 people you love. In Gaza, one of them has been killed." 29 solid figures and 1 red outline.

### Hospital
**Current presentation:** shelf of pills and clinic icons; evacuation bars on the data page.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 42 of 670 health facilities fully working (Health Cluster dashboard, 3 Oct 2026); 46% of essential medicines at zero stock (WHO, 4 Mar 2026).
- **Context:** 18,500 patients needed care no longer available in Gaza (WHO, Feb 2026); 1,092 died waiting (Jul 2024 – Nov 2025). On WHO-arranged missions, Egypt took 4,214 patients, the UK 53, the US 28 (Health Cluster, 27 Sep 2026).
- **Implication:** "The scan, the chemo, the dialysis your mother would get next week at home. In Gaza she waits, and often dies waiting."

### Water
**Current presentation:** data page only.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 31% of assessed households below 15 litres per person per day (WASH Cluster, Aug 2026), against 142 litres for the average person in the UK (Water UK).
- **Context:** 15 litres is the emergency minimum for drinking, cooking and washing combined; 57% fell below the 6-litre drinking minimum.
- **Implication:** "Your child's bath, her bottle, the dishes. In Gaza, a family chooses which of those happens today."

### Wheelchairs
**Current presentation:** needed vs delivered rows.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** 12,146 of 32,835 mobility aids delivered, Jan 2025 – May 2026 (WHO, May 2026).
- **Context:** 43,011 people with life-changing injuries; 502 of 2,277 assessed amputees fitted with a limb.
- **Implication:** "If your son lost a leg, he would most likely still be waiting for the wheelchair or the leg."

### Bombs and land
**Current presentation:** 6,000 bombs vs Afghanistan; land squares.
**Optimized presentation:**
- **Stat & source:** about 6,000 bombs in six days (Israeli Air Force, 12 Oct 2023); 40,000+ targets in year one (Israeli military); less than 40% of land reachable (OCHA, Sep 2026).
- **Context:** US forces released 7,423 weapons across Afghanistan in all of 2019.
- **Implication:** "A territory the length of a marathon took almost a year of a whole country's war in less than a week."
- **To verify:** the 7,423 (US AFCENT, 2019) and the ~4,000 tonnes figure were not re-checked against a primary source this session.

## 2. Hero metric layout

**Chosen metric:** 21,283 children killed and identified by name. It is the most human, the least disputable (names, not estimates), and it anchors every later chapter about "your child".

- A full-width night-black band at the very top, nothing else in it.
- The number set in the display serif at roughly 230px on desktop and 96px on phones, white on near-black.
- One line beneath it, with "identified by name" in italic rose.
- Beneath that, a canvas drawing 21,283 individual dots, filling in over 2.6 seconds on load. The size of the wall *is* the number.
- A single caption: 851 classrooms of 25, plus the UN's "four months vs four years" comparison.
- Generous margins, no tiles and no other numbers competing in the band.

## 3. Anchoring (all derived from the sourced figures)

| Raw figure | Anchor | Arithmetic |
|---|---|---|
| 21,283 children | 851 classrooms of 25 | 21,283 ÷ 25 = 851.3 |
| 73,922 killed of 2.2 million | 1 in 30 people | 2,200,000 ÷ 73,922 = 29.8 |
| 563 kindergartens, 86% lost | fewer than 1 in 7 left | 14% ≈ 1 in 7.1 |
| 154 weeks without school | vs 19 weeks in England | 7 Oct 2023 → 19 Sep 2026 = 1,078 days |
| 657,200 children | close to every pupil in Scotland | 657,200 ÷ 695,923 = 94% |
| 76.6% homes hit | 2.3 of 10 homes on your street undamaged | 10 × 0.234 |
| 42 of 670 facilities | 6 in 100 | 42 ÷ 670 = 6.3% |
| 32,835 needed, 12,146 delivered | fewer than 4 in 10 | 12,146 ÷ 32,835 = 37% |
| 6,000 bombs in 6 days | one every 86 seconds | 518,400 s ÷ 6,000 = 86.4 |

## 4. Wireframe and UI recommendations (as built)

- **Chapter pattern:** label ("Your daughter's first classroom") → serif headline with the two figures in colour (blue = your world, red = Gaza) → the picture → three-column text (The stat · What it measures · What it means).
- **The picture:** two stacked rows of large Phosphor icons, 10 per row and up to 72px each. The top row is the familiar yardstick and the bottom row is Gaza. Partial icons are cut to the exact decimal.
- **Time comparisons:** one dot per week (19 vs 154), so the gap is felt as length.
- **Distribution:** a single ranked bar list for who took the sick, with Western countries in blue so the small bars are the point.
- **Motion:** each picture redraws from empty when it scrolls into view and rests fully drawn. Reduced-motion users see the final state.
- **Avoiding dashboard sterility:** no KPI tile grids, no gauges. Thin rules instead of cards, one idea per screen-height, serif headlines, and human-scale nouns (baby, backpack, house, person, drop) rather than abstract bars.
- **Fairness:** "What Israel says" fold-outs remain beside contested chapters. They are what makes the page hard to dismiss.

## 5. Action: write to the companies

- Every company card has a red "Write to them" button. It opens a composer with the company pre-selected.
- The letter is factual and courteous. It states the company's documented role and evidence, cites the UN figures, the ICJ order and the UN Commission of Inquiry finding, and asks three questions under the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.
- Buttons: Copy letter, Open their contact page, Open in my email.
- Verified contact pages: Caterpillar, Boeing, RTX, Rheinmetall, General Dynamics (media contacts). Other companies link to their official website.
- No company logos are drawn; names, home-country flags and evidence identify them.
